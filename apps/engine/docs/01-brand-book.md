## 0. The naming decision (read this first)

### The insight that decided it
Every receptionist in every clinic in India hears the same sentence, 40 times a day:

> **"Doctor sahab, meri baari kab aayegi?"**
> **"Doctor ji, mera number kab aayega?"**

That sentence *is* the entire product. Our brand should be the answer to it, not a description of our tech stack.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Tokenji (टोकनजी) | "Token" is already the word clinics use; "-ji" = instant Indian trust. Cute, friendly, WhatsApp-native. | 9 | 9 | 8 | 7 | **86** |

### ✅ FINAL: **Baari**

- **Wordmark:** `baari` (always lowercase, never "Baari Technologies" in front of a customer)
- **Spoken form:** "Baari" (BAA-ree). Call it **"Baari app"** in Hinglish, **"Baari system"** for older owners.
- **Descriptor (for GST invoice, website, app stores):** *Baari — Smart Queue & WhatsApp Automation for Clinics, Labs & Salons*
- **Product family (one product, three modes — never three products):**
  - 🩺 **Baari Clinic** — doctors/patients/tokens
  - 🧪 **Baari Lab** — tests/home collection/reports
  - ✂️ **Baari Salon** — stylists/clients/slots

**Fallback if a Class 9/42 trademark clash appears in your search:** `Baaribook` → `Tokenji` (in that order). Never fall back to an English "-ly / -sync / -hq" name; it kills the Tier-3 trust advantage.

## 1. Positioning

**Category we are NOT in:** clinic management software / HIS / CRM / SaaS dashboard.
That category is crowded (Practo Ray ₹999–₹1,999/mo, MocDoc, BestoSys), price-anchored low, and owners are bored of it.

**Category we own:** **"Front-desk automation"** — the 20 metres between the clinic door and the doctor's cabin, sold as *staff you don't have to hire*.

### One-line positioning
> **Baari is the ₹1,999/month receptionist that never sleeps** — it answers every "mera number kab aayega?" on WhatsApp, calls patients in by name on your TV, quotes lab tests from a photo of the prescription, and gets you 5-star Google reviews on autopilot.

### Positioning statement (internal)
For **single-to-mid-size clinics, diagnostic labs and salons in Indian Tier-1/2/3 cities** who **lose patients, staff time and reviews to a chaotic walk-in queue and unanswered WhatsApp**, **Baari** is a **zero-app, zero-hardware WhatsApp front desk** that **gives every patient a live token on their own phone and calls them in on the TV they already own**. Unlike **Practo/MocDoc-style practice software**, Baari **takes no commission, shows no competitor ads, requires no patient app, no new hardware and no training beyond four buttons**.

### The three anchoring comparisons (use in this order, always)
| Their current reality | Cost | Our story |
|---|---|---|
| LED token display board (hardware vendor) | **₹30,000–₹80,000 one-time** + AMC + wiring + a remote that gets lost | "Your existing TV becomes the token board. ₹0 extra." |
| Practo / marketplace bookings | ₹999–₹1,999/mo **+ 6–12% commission** + competitors advertised to your own patients | "No commission. No ads. Your patients stay yours." |
| A second receptionist for evening shift | ₹9,000–₹14,000/month | "₹1,999/month, works 24×7, never takes leave." |

### Pricing posture
**Hidden public pricing.** Website shows only: *"48-hour VIP pilot — ₹1,500, fully adjustable"*. Prices are quoted in person, after the demo. This protects the ₹7.5k–₹25k setup band from being shopped on price and keeps you in a "solution sale" rather than a "software subscription" fight with Practo.

## 2. Message architecture

### Primary tagline (Hindi/Hinglish)
> ### **"Aapki baari, WhatsApp par."**
> *(Your turn, on WhatsApp.)*

### Support lines by vertical
| Mode | Line |
|---|---|
| 🩺 Clinic | **"Waiting room ki tension khatam. Patient ko khud pata chalega — 2 log pehle."** |
| 🧪 Lab | **"Prescription ki photo bhejo — price, fasting aur ghar se sample, sab WhatsApp par."** |
| ✂️ Salon | **"Client ki baari, bina ek bhi call."** |
| Universal hero (ads/posters) | **"MERI BAARI KAB AAYEGI?"** printed huge, and underneath, small: **"Ab Baari bata degi."** |
| English (website footer / investor) | *The queue that tells patients when it's their turn.* |

### The "No" list — words we never use in front of a customer
`SaaS · multi-tenant · dashboard · CRM · workflow · onboarding · platform · solution · AI-powered · cloud-based · API · integration · seat · license · sync`
→ Replace with: `system · line · TV · WhatsApp · token · reception · aapka clinic · 4 button`

### The "Yes" list — words that sell
`baari · token · line · 2 log pehle · TV pe naam · WhatsApp pe · ghar se · report · review · reception wali didi · compounder · bina app · bina hardware`

### Voice & tone
- **Warm, second person, Hinglish-first.** "Aapka clinic", not "your organization".
- **Confident, never technical.** Explain outcomes, never mechanisms.
- **Never shame the staff.** The receptionist is the hero of the story, not the problem. ("Reception wali didi ka kaam aasaan ho jayega" — she is your internal champion; lose her and you lose the account.)
- **Numbers beat adjectives.** "22 minute average wait" not "efficient queue management".

## 3. Visual identity

### Colour system
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Baari Teal** | `#0B7A6B` | Primary brand, headers, TV "NOW SERVING" background |
| **Token Amber** | `#F2A63B` | Token numbers, "currently serving" highlight, primary CTA accents |
| **Ink** | `#0F1E1B` | Text, TV base |
| **Mint Mist** | `#E6F4F1` | App surfaces, cards, table zebra |
| **Paper** | `#FBF8F2` | Warm off-white background — prints cheaply on standees, feels less "hospital" |
| **WhatsApp Green** | `#25D366` | **Only** for WhatsApp affordances (buttons that send a WA message). Never as a brand colour — we are not claiming to be WhatsApp. |
| **Alert Red** | `#D64545` | Emergency jump, no-show, 1–3★ private alerts |

Deliberate choice: teal, not the default "medical blue" (#1E88E5, used by every clinic software) and not WhatsApp green. Teal reads *calm, clean, trustworthy* on a cheap LED TV and on a ₹6 printed sticker.

### Type
| Role | Font (all free, Google Fonts + system fallback) |
|---|---|
| Display / wordmark | **Plus Jakarta Sans** (700/800), fallback `system-ui` |
| UI / body | **Inter** (400/500/600) |
| Devanagari | **Mukta** (or Noto Sans Devanagari) |
| Gurmukhi (Punjab!) | **Noto Sans Gurmukhi** |
| TV display numbers | **Inter Tight / Tabular numerals** — token numbers must be **tabular** so they don't jitter |

> Ship fonts self-hosted (woff2) — clinic internet is unreliable and Google Fonts CDN is blocked on some hospital networks.

### Logo — 3 concepts, one recommendation
- **A. "Token Dot"** — lowercase `baari`; the dot on the `i` is a rounded token badge holding a number that can animate `1 → 2 → 3`. *Cheapest to produce, works at 16px favicon and on a 55″ TV.*
- **B. "Queue B"** — a `b` built from three stacked discs (tokens), top disc amber. Distinctive but fiddly at small sizes.
- **C. "Bubble Token"** — a speech bubble with a queue notch cut into the tail, holding a `#`. *Best standalone app icon.*

**Recommendation:** **C as the app icon/social avatar + A as the wordmark.** Lockup: `[C mark] baari` horizontal, with tagline only in the hero, never in the lockup.

### TV display design rules (this is a *brand surface*, not a settings page)
- Legible from **6 metres** in a bright waiting room: token number minimum **14% of screen height**.
- Only **3 things** on screen: `NOW SERVING`, the number, and the cabin/doctor. Everything else is secondary and dimmed.
- No logos of our vendors. No "Powered by AI". The clinic's own logo sits top-left — **we are white-label by default and monetise it** (custom TV skin = Pro tier).
- Announcement chime: soft, 2-note, never a hospital beep.

### Physical brand assets (the stuff that actually closes clinics)
1. **A5 acrylic/foam QR standee** — "Scan karo, baari pao 🎫" + clinic name + Hindi line. Cost ₹35–₹60 printed, sells for perception of ₹500.
2. **Counter sticker** (4″ circle) — "Baari yahan se shuru hoti hai".
3. **TV caption card** — a small acrylic below the TV: "Yeh TV aapka token board hai".
4. **Receptionist card** — credit-card size, 4 buttons with icons in Hindi. *This card is the entire training programme.*
5. **Leave-behind A5** — 6 screenshots, no features, 3 prices. Printed on 300gsm, matte.

## 4. Brand architecture & legal housekeeping

- **One brand, three modes** — the mode switcher changes *labels*, not the product. Never launch "Baari Lab" as a separate site; it fragments your SEO and your story.
- **Completely separate from the factory/IndiaMART business**: separate entity (Pvt Ltd or LLP), separate GSTIN, separate bank account, separate Google Workspace, separate Supabase project, separate Meta app, separate Razorpay account. No shared logos, no shared WhatsApp numbers, no shared support email. (Also matters for DPDP Act 2023 data-fiduciary boundaries and for a future fundraise.)
- **Suggested entity name:** *Baari Systems Pvt Ltd* (descriptor on invoices: "Software & front-desk automation services" for GST clarity on the setup fee vs subscription split).
- **Data promise (print it on the leave-behind):** *"Aapka patient data sirf aapka. Har clinic ka data alag, encrypted, export anytime. DPDP Act 2023 compliant. Hum aapke patients ko kabhi ads nahi bhejte."* — this is a real differentiator against marketplaces.

## 5. What "brand consistency" means for a 3-person company

Do these five things on every surface, and you will look bigger than you are:
1. Teal `#0B7A6B` + amber token numbers. Everywhere. Even on the ₹6 sticker.
2. The words **baari** and **token** in every headline. Never "queue management".
3. Every screen ends with a **human confirmation in Hinglish**: `Token 18 bhej diya ✓` — never `Success: 200 OK`.
4. Show a **real TV screen** in every demo, on their own TV, with their own clinic name on it. That 12-second moment closes more deals than any brochure.
5. The **same 4 buttons** on every device. Reception is the product.
