### 1.2 What the setup fee actually *is* (never call it a "setup charge")
Print this on the leave-behind as a deliverables list — it converts the fee from "tax" into "work":
1. On-site installation of the TV display on their existing TV (30–60 min).
2. QR standee + 6 counter stickers printed and placed. *(Cost you ₹80, charge inside ₹14,999.)*
3. WhatsApp connection, warm-up and test on the clinic's own number.
4. Catalogue + doctor list + timings configured **by you**, not by them.
5. On-site 60-second training for receptionist + doctor (**you do the taps in front of them**).
6. First 50 patients migrated / first day supervised live at the desk.
7. Their Google Maps link wired into the review shield.

### 1.3 Payment terms
- **60% advance, 40% on go-live** (day 1 acceptance). UPI QR shown on the tablet at the moment of yes.
- **Monthly AMC by UPI Autopay / Razorpay subscription** — never chase cash monthly. If they refuse autopay, take annual upfront (push annual hard: it kills churn and funds you).
- GST: 18% on both (software services). Quote **inclusive** prices to SMBs — a ₹14,999 that becomes ₹17,699 at the till kills momentum. Absorb it into the headline number (i.e. ₹14,999 incl. GST, or explicitly label "+18% GST" — pick one and be consistent; recommendation: **quote ex-GST with GST shown only on the invoice**, because doctors claim input credit and respect transparency).
- **No discounting the setup fee. Ever.** Concession ladder, in order: free 2nd month → free extra TV screen → free Lab module trial 30 days → free standee branding → annual at 3 months free. Discounting setup re-prices your whole market (Ludhiana doctors all talk to each other at IDA meets).

### 1.4 The 48-hour VIP Pilot (your door-opener)
- **₹1,500, fully adjustable** against any plan.
- 48 hours live, on **their** number, with **their** real patients, up to 30 tokens.
- Includes: QR standee, TV display installed, receptionist trained, you personally on WhatsApp for those 2 days.
- **Refund if they don't convert**, no questions, and they keep the standee.
- Why this works: the doctor cannot evaluate software by looking at it. He *can* evaluate "the receptionist stopped getting 30 calls today". The pilot manufactures that evidence.

## 2. Who to sell to in Ludhiana (build the list this week)

### 2.1 Target profile — buy on day one if you find these
**🩺 Clinic (highest) :** 1–3 doctors, walk-in heavy, one receptionist/compounder, an LED "now serving" board already on the wall or a whiteboard with numbers, 40+ patients/day, and a doctor who is *personally* on the phone. **Already has a token system = already believes in queueing = easiest sale in the city.**
**🧪 Lab (highest ticket):** does home collection with 2–4 phlebotomists, receives prescription photos on WhatsApp today, sends PDFs manually.
**✂️ Salon (fastest close, lowest ticket):** 3–8 chairs, takes bookings on Instagram DMs + phone, Sarabha Nagar / Mall Road / BRS Nagar belt, ₹1,200+ average ticket (they can afford ₹1,999).

### 2.2 Geography — cluster your walking days
| Day | Belt | Anchor points |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Sarabha Nagar + BRS Nagar** | Salon/spa density, premium dental clinics, Verka/market area |
| Tue | **Model Town + Gurdev Nagar** | Multi-specialty polyclinics, skin/eye clinics |
| Wed | **Ferozepur Road + Kitchlu Nagar** | Dental belt, nursing homes, DMC/CMC catchment clinics |
| Thu | **Dugri + Pakhowal Road + Haibowal** | Labs, pathologists, physio, salons |
| Fri | **Civil Lines + Miller Ganj + Shimlapuri** | Chemists, doctors' chambers, older GP practices |
| Sat | **Chandigarh Road / Jalandhar Bypass / Sahnewal** | Manufacturing-belt clinics + salons near GT Road |

**Channels to enumerate (free):** Google Maps search "dental clinic near me" per locality → export name+phone; Justdial/Sulekha listings; **IDA Punjab / Ludhiana Dental Association** member lists and their monthly CME; local chemist and medical-store owners (they know every clinic and will refer for ₹1,000/₹2,000 — best channel in Tier-2); beauty-product distributors (they visit every salon weekly — pay them ₹1,500 per closed salon); Instagram location tags (#ludhianasalon, geotag "Sarabha Nagar") for salons.

### 2.3 Time of day (this matters more than your script)
| Segment | Best door time | Worst |
|---|---|---|
| Clinics | **2:00–4:30 PM** (post-OPD lull) and 8:30–9:30 PM (closing, owner alone) | 9–12 AM, 6–8 PM |
| Labs | 11 AM–1 PM (post morning collection) | 6–9 AM |
| Salons | **Mon–Tue 11 AM–1 PM** (many closed Mon; check) | Sat/Sun 4–8 PM |
| Doctors at home / CME | Sunday morning association meets | — |

**Rule: never demo to a receptionist. Demo to the owner, or leave and come back.** The receptionist is not the buyer — but she is the *blocker*. Handle her first with the 60-second card and a chai, then ask her to introduce you.

## 3. The pitch

### 3.1 The 12-second opening (walk-in, standing at reception)
> **You:** "Sir, ek line bol ke jaata hoon — phir aap decide karein. Aapke reception pe din me kitni baar phone aata hai *'mera number kab aayega'*?"
> **Doctor:** "Bahut aata hai…" *(he always says this)*
> **You:** "Hum wahi band karte hain. Patient ko WhatsApp pe khud pata chal jaata hai — token number, kitna time lagega, aur jab 2 log pehle bachein to message: 'nikal aaiye'. Aapki TV bhi token board ban jaati hai. Do minute ka demo hai, aapke clinic ke naam se, iss phone pe?"

**If he says "abhi busy hoon":** "Bilkul sir, 2 baje aata hoon — 10 minute. Aapka naam aur clinic ka naam bata dein, main demo pehle se aapke naam se bana ke laata hoon." *(Get the name. The personalised demo is the whole trick.)*

### 3.2 The pre-loaded demo (do this before every visit — non-negotiable)
Before you walk in, create a tenant named `Dr. Sharma Dental, Model Town` in your demo workspace and **issue a token on your own phone** so the WhatsApp thread already exists. Then, sitting in front of him:

1. **Show the WhatsApp thread** (30 s): *"Yeh message aapke clinic ke naam se ja raha hai."* Let him hold your phone.
2. **Ask him to speak a voice note** in Punjabi/Hindi: *"Dr. Sharma ko aaj shaam dikhana hai, daant me dard hai."* Bot replies with a token **in 4 seconds**. Watch his face. *(This is the moment. Do not talk over it.)*
3. **Cast the TV** to the nearest screen (or his TV in the waiting room) with the pairing code. Token appears. **Audio announces in Hindi.**
4. **Tap `✅ CALL NEXT`** and let him watch the TV change + his own phone buzz.
5. **Tap `⏸ +15 min`** and show the WhatsApp broadcast going to all waiting patients. *"Aapka compounder ab 30 phone calls nahi karega."*
6. *(Labs only)* Send a photo of a **real handwritten prescription** — his own, from his desk — and show the quote come back with tests + fasting + price + GPS pin request.

Total: **4 minutes.** Then stop. Do not show the dashboard. Do not show settings.

### 3.3 The price frame (say it exactly in this order)
> "Sir, market me LED token board lagwane ka ₹50,000 lagta hai — aur usme WhatsApp nahi hai, reminder nahi hai, review nahi hai. Baari ka poora setup **₹14,999** hai — one time, aapki TV pe, aapke WhatsApp pe. Uske baad **₹1,999 mahina** — din ka ₹66. Aapka ek patient no-show hua to ₹500 gaya. Mahine me 4 no-show = ₹2,000 — matlab fees free ki free nikal aati hai. Aur setup me main khud aake TV, standee, staff training — sab karunga."

Then **shut up.** Let him do the arithmetic.

### 3.4 The close (two options, both easy)
> **A (fast):** "Aaj QR standee laga dete hain, 48 ghante chala kar dekhiye — 20 patient. ₹1,500, plan me adjust ho jayega, pasand na aaye to wapas. Aapke number pe hi chalega."
> **B (direct):** "Aaj ₹9,000 de dein, main shuru kar deta hoon, baaki go-live pe. Kal shaam tak TV aur WhatsApp dono chalu."

Never leave without one of: **(a) a paid pilot, (b) a booked demo slot with the owner in his calendar, or (c) a specific reason + a next date.** "Main sochke batata hoon" = you failed to get a next step. Convert it: *"Bilkul sir — main kal 2 baje do minute ke liye aa jaunga, bas ek screenshot dikhana hai ki aapke area ke kitne patients WhatsApp pe aate hain."*

## 5. The 90-day plan

### Phase 0 — Week 1: build the weapon, not the pipeline
- Deploy tenant #0 = **your own demo clinic** with a live Baileys number, TV link, 6 sample tokens.
- Print 100 QR standees, 200 counter stickers, 200 A5 leave-behinds, 100 receptionist cards. (≈ ₹8–12k. This is your best money spent.)
- Shoot **6 vertical videos (30 s each)** on a real clinic TV: token issuing, voice note, 2-ahead alert, TV announcement, lab quote from a photo, review shield. These are what you WhatsApp after every visit.
- Build the list: **300 targets** in a sheet (name, area, segment, phone, owner name, best time, status).

### Phase 1 — Week 2–4: 20 walk-ins/day is the whole job
Weekly target: **40 quality walk-ins, 15 owner conversations, 8 demos, 3 pilots, 2 paid.**
Daily rhythm: 10:00 list + 5 follow-ups → 11:30–1:00 walk-ins (labs/salons) → 2:00–4:30 clinics → 4:30–6:00 demos/installations → 7:00–9:00 follow-up calls to today's "sochta hoon" + WhatsApp video → 9:00 update sheet.
**Rule:** every pilot must go live within 24h of payment or refund it — a stalled pilot is a lost account and a damaged reference.

### Phase 2 — Week 5–8: turn 8 tenants into a machine
- **Referral programme:** ₹2,000 cash or 2 months free, per converted referral, paid *after* the referred clinic's first month. Ask at day 30, when the review shield has just got them 5 new 5★ reviews.
- **Association play:** sponsor one IDA Ludhiana CME (₹5–15k) and demo on the projector to 60 doctors. Best ROI in the city.
- **Channel partners:** 10 chemists + 5 beauty distributors, 20% of first-year value, tracked by a unique code.
- **Case-study asset:** pick your best dental + best salon, shoot a 90-second founder-to-doctor video. Every subsequent pitch opens with it.
- Hire **1 field person** (₹18–25k + ₹1,500/closed deal) once you're at 10 tenants.

### Phase 3 — Week 9–13: raise price, add lab module
- Once you have 15 logos, **stop discounting and raise Growth to ₹19,999 setup**. Counter-intuitive but correct: your market judges quality by price, and your first 15 got a founder's discount they'll happily tell others about.
- Push **Lab module** into every existing clinic tenant (₹9,999 + ₹1,499) — highest margin upsell you own.
- Target state at day 90: **25 tenants · ₹3.5L setup collected · ₹45–55k MRR · 90%+ gross margin · <5% churn.**

### Funnel math you should hold yourself to
```
300 names in list
 → 160 doors knocked (53%)
 →  55 owner conversations (34% of knocks)
 →  30 demos shown        (55%)
 →  12 pilots started     (40%)
 →   8 paid               (67% pilot→paid)
 →   2 referrals          (25%)
= ~10 tenants / 45 days per rep
```
If `demo → pilot` is below 40%, your demo is too long or you're demoing to the receptionist. If `pilot → paid` is below 60%, your first-day handholding is weak (you are not physically at the desk on day 1).

## 7. What you never do
- Never show the admin dashboard, settings or pricing page in a first demo.
- Never sell to the receptionist, and never let her feel replaced. **She is your champion** — frame it as "aapki 30 call kam ho jayengi, aapki value badh jayegi".
- Never promise "100% no-shows khatam" or "WhatsApp kabhi block nahi hoga". Promise the mechanism, not the miracle.
- Never run a live clinic's queue from the same WhatsApp number as another city's demo.
- Never take cash without a GST invoice; never take a cheque from a clinic that hasn't gone live.
- Never discount the setup fee. Give months, screens or modules instead.
- Never leave a pilot un-installed overnight.
